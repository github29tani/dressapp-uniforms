## ❌ Missing Data NOT in Excel File

The Excel file contains **ONLY product data**. The following school information needs to be added manually or from another source:

### School Profile Information
- ❌ **School Location** (city, state, address)
- ❌ **School Contact** (phone, email, website)
- ❌ **School Logo/Image**
- ❌ **School Description**
- ❌ **School Code** (unique identifier)
- ❌ **Principal Name**
- ❌ **Board Affiliation** (CBSE, ICSE, State Board, etc.)
- ❌ **Established Year**
- ❌ **Student Strength**

### Additional Features You Might Want
- ❌ **School Timings**
- ❌ **Uniform Change Dates** (Summer/Winter)
- ❌ **Special Instructions** (e.g., "Girls must wear white socks")
- ❌ **Embroidery Requirements** (school monogram, house badges)
- ❌ **Approved Vendors List**
- ❌ **Size Chart Images** specific to each school
